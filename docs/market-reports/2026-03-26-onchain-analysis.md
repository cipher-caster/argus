# BTC On-Chain Analysis — 2026-03-26

## Context

BTC price: ~$68,606 | Weekly EMA50: $88,671 | Regime: BEAR (-22.6% below EMA50)
Question: Is the bottom in, or is there more downside?

---

## MVRV Z-Score: 0.49–0.55 (Neutral)

The key "overvalued vs undervalued" metric. Historical cycle bottoms occur when MVRV Z-Score
goes **negative** (red zone, typically -0.5 to -1.0). At 0.49–0.55 we are neutral — not
overvalued, but not at the deep capitulation level that has historically marked cycle bottoms.

**Signal: Not bottomed. More downside possible before entering classic buy zone.**

Source: [BingX — MVRV Z-Score 0.49 early March 2026](https://bingx.com/en/news/post/bitcoin-risk-meter-shows-deeply-negative-sharpe-as-mvrv-z-score-sits-at-in-early-march)

## Long-Term Holder (LTH) Behavior: Encouraging

- LTH realized price: ~$45,200 — BTC at $68K is well above LTH cost basis, no panic selling
- LTH selling pressure dropped **87%** from February to March:
  - Feb 5: -243,737 BTC/month (heavy distribution)
  - Mar 1: -31,967 BTC/month (near zero)
- LTH supply still near cycle highs — holders are staying put, not exiting

**Signal: LTHs not capitulating. Positive — but not a confirmed bottom signal on its own.**

Source: [BingX — LTH Realized Supply Near Peak](https://bingx.com/en/news/post/bitcoin-long-term-holder-realized-supply-stays-near-peak-as-march-metrics-cool)

## Supply In Profit vs Loss: Convergence Not Yet Reached

- Currently: 11.1M BTC in profit vs 8.9M BTC in loss
- Historical bottoms form when these two measures **converge**
- Convergence would imply a spot price near **~$60,000**
- We are not there yet

**Signal: Watch $60K as a key convergence/capitulation level.**

---

## Whale Accumulation: Positive

- Wallets holding 10–10,000 BTC accumulated ~91,000 BTC over the past 90 days
- This follows heavy dumping throughout January and February
- One day saw ~31,900 BTC (~$3B) withdrawn from exchanges — one of the largest outflow events of 2026

**Signal: Smart money quietly accumulating. Historically precedes bottoms but timing is uncertain.**

---

## Fear & Greed Index: 15 (Extreme Fear)

Classic contrarian indicator at the extremes. However, extreme fear can persist for months
in a bear cycle — this alone is not a timing signal.

## Conclusion

**The bottom is likely not in.** The data points to continued downside toward **$55–65K**
before a confirmed bottom, with late 2026 as the consensus window.

Positive leading indicators (LTH selling collapsing, whale accumulation) suggest we are in
the **accumulation phase leading into the bottom**, not past it. This is consistent with
Argus maintaining BEAR regime and trading SHORT-only.

### Key Levels to Watch
- **$60,000** — profit/loss supply convergence zone; historically a strong capitulation signal
- **$50,000–$55,000** — analyst consensus bottom range (Oct–Dec 2026)
- **$45,200** — LTH realized price; below this, long-term holders are underwater (panic risk)
- **$88,671** — BTC weekly EMA50; reclaiming this flips Argus regime to BULL

### For Argus Trading
- Keep BEAR regime active, SHORT-only signals are correct
- Do not go long until BTC reclaims weekly EMA50 ($88,671)
- If BTC approaches $60K, watch for profit/loss convergence — potential bottom signal
- Re-run this analysis when regime approaches a flip
