# Market Health (The Pulse)

**Market Health** provides a high-level physical for the entire crypto market. It ignores individual signals and focuses on Breadth and Structure.

## 1. Trend Health (The 200 EMA Breadth)

This metric answers: _"Is it a Bull Market or a Bear Market?"_
It counts what percentage of coins are trading **Above** their 200-Day EMA.

- **> 60% Bullish**: **Confirmed Bull Market**. Aggressive strategies (Breakouts, Leverage) work best.
- **< 40% Bullish**: **Bear Market**. Defensive strategies (Shorts, Cash, Mean Reversion) required.
- **40% - 60%**: **Transition/Chop**. Most trend-following bots fail here. Reduce size.

## 2. Volatility Regimes

Volatility measures how much price is moving. We categorize the market into three regimes based on normalized ATR (Average True Range):

### ☢️ DANGER (High Volatility)

- **Logic**: Average ATR > 2.0% of price per candle.
- **Meaning**: Panic dumping or Euphoric pumping.
- **Action**: **Wide Stops**. Position sizing must be reduced to account for larger swings.

### ⚡ ACTIVE (Healthy Volatility)

- **Logic**: Average ATR between 0.5% and 2.0%.
- **Meaning**: Normal market conditions.
- **Action**: **Standard Risk**. Technical Analysis is most respected in this environment.

### 🛡️ STABLE (Low Volatility)

- **Logic**: Average ATR < 0.5%.
- **Meaning**: The market is asleep.
- **Action**: **Stay Cash**. Do not anticipate moves. Wait for expansion ("The Squeeze").

## 3. The Squeeze

Tracks the percentage of coins with Bollinger Bandwidth < 0.1.

- **High Squeeze Pct**: A massive explosive move is imminent. Be ready.
