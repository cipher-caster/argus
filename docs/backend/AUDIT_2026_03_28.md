**Critical — Silent swallowing:**
- `routes/analytics.py:231` — `except Exception: pass` on market regime detection (ALL best-setups fail silently)
- `routes/analytics.py:326` — `except Exception:` on MTF confirmation, no logging
- `providers/okx_provider.py:89` — `except Exception: return None` in `get_ticker_price()`

- `worker.py:266` — `except Exception: pass` in shutdown

**Critical — Raw exception leaks:**
- `routes/strategy.py:181,220,297` — `detail=str(e)` on 500s

- `routes/indicators.py:147,153,244,247` — str(e) leaks

- `trading/orchestrator.py:244-284` — `check_pending_fills()` no try-catch on session.execute
