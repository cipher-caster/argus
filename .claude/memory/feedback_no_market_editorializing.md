Stick to what the data shows. Don't editorialize market cycle stage or price direction beyond what the indicators actually say.

**Why:** User pushed back when I called it a "late-stage bear market" — that was my interpretation, not something the Argus data supports. The system tracks current regime and signals, not cycle position.

**How to apply:** In market reports, distinguish clearly between data observations ("BTC is 22.6% below weekly EMA50, slope falling") and speculation ("we're near the bottom"). If asked for a macro view, give the data and be explicit that cycle position is outside what Argus can determine. Recommend on-chain tools (Glassnode, CryptoQuant) for that layer.
