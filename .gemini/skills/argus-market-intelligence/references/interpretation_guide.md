# Argus Interpretation Guide

Use this guide to translate raw Argus API data into structured intelligence.

## Oracle Earnest Score
- **+3 to +4 (STRONG BUY)**: High conviction long. Reclaim of major levels.
- **+1 to +2 (BUY)**: Moderate bullish. Price above EMA20/50 but below major resistance.
- **0 (NEUTRAL)**: No edge. Likely ranging or preparing for a volatile move.
- **-1 to -2 (SELL)**: Moderate bearish. Price below key EMAs.
- **-3 to -4 (STRONG SELL)**: High conviction short. Structure broken.

## Titan Signals & Confidence
- **BUY / SELL**: Immediate market entry. Confidence > 60% is tradeable.
- **BUY_LIMIT / SELL_LIMIT**: Wait for pullback to entry price.
- **Confidence (80%)**: Strong conviction; full size.
- **Confidence (60%)**: Moderate conviction; half size.
- **Confidence (0%)**: Low/Warmup; avoid or paper trade only.

## MTF Confluence (Eliz + Mayne Framework)
- **Eliz Lane (Execution)**: 4H entry trigger + 1D swing structure.
- **Mayne Lane (Macro)**: 12H bias + 1W macro trend.
- **Confluence Scoring**:
  - **4 Confirmed**: Elite Confluence (100% size).
  - **2–3 Confirmed**: Tradeable but size down (50% size).
  - **0–1 Confirmed**: High risk; wait for setup to mature.

## Risk/Reward Rules
- **Minimum RR**: 1.5:1 to TP1.
- **Target RR**: 2:1 or higher for optimal equity curve.
- **Tight RR (< 1.5:1)**: Note as "tight"; consider waiting for better entry or deeper pullback.

## Market States & ADX
- **TRENDING**: Ride the trend with trailing stops.
- **RANGING (ADX < 20)**: Avoid chase entries; use tighter TP targets.
- **VOLATILE**: High ATR; reduce position size and widen stop-loss levels.
- **SUPER TREND**: Maximum directional strength; prioritize this direction exclusively.
